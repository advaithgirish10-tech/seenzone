    Privacy policy

    Last updated 5 October 2026

    The short version

    Your chat is read and analysed only inside your own browser. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else, never stored, and disappears when you close or reload the page. We have no account system, no database, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.

    What happens to your chat

    When you choose an export file, your browser reads it into memory, counts things (messages, reply times, words and so on) and draws the results on your screen. No part of the file, the messages, the names or the results is sent over the network. Because we never receive your chat, we cannot see it, keep it, sell it or hand it to anyone.

    What the website itself loads

    The page is a single file. Its fonts and code are bundled inside it, so it makes no requests to Google, font services, code libraries or any other third party while you use it.

    Hosting

    Like every website, the server that delivers this page to you (our hosting provider) automatically receives technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, and may keep it briefly in server logs for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or profile anyone. They never include your chat, because your chat never leaves your device.

    Sharing

    Nothing is shared unless you choose to. "Share to Story" creates an image on your device and hands it to your phone's share sheet. "Send to them on WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message. Once you share something, it is governed by the app you shared it to. You can turn on "Hide names and quoted messages" before sharing.

    Other people in your chat

    A chat includes messages from your friend. Only use chats you are part of, and do not publish anything about another person that they would not be comfortable with.

    Children

    This site is not intended for children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law where you live). We do not knowingly receive any personal data from anyone, including children.

    Your rights

    Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you have a question about privacy or a request under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or California privacy law, contact us and we will respond.
